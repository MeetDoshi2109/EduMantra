FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is EduMantra?
EduMantra is an AI-enabled Skill Intelligence and Learning Platform for officials in India's Official Statistical System. It assesses competencies, identifies skill gaps, and recommends personalised learning pathways from iGOT Karmayogi and NSSTA TPAC programmes.
How does the skill gap analysis work?
When you create your profile (designation, education, experience, job role), our AI engine maps your competencies against predefined frameworks for Official Statistics. It compares your current level to requirements and generates a detailed gap report with severity ratings and LLM-powered recommendations.
Is EduMantra integrated with iGOT Karmayogi?
Yes. EduMantra directly integrates with iGOT Karmayogi. You can browse the catalogue, enrol directly, track progress and have completions reflected in your competency profile � all from within EduMantra.
What languages does the platform support?
EduMantra supports English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali and Marathi. The AI assistant responds in your preferred language, making it accessible to officials across all states.
How does the AI MCQ generator work?
Educators can upload documents or presentations. Our AI analyses the content and generates multiple-choice questions with options, correct answers, difficulty levels and detailed explanations � ready to publish as an assessment in minutes.
Is my data secure?
Yes. EduMantra uses Supabase with Row Level Security (RLS) so each user can only access their own data. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. The platform supports role-based access control, SSO, and maintains full audit logs.
Who can use EduMantra?
EduMantra is designed for five roles: Students/Officials (learners), Educators/Instructors (trainers), Supervisors (performance monitoring), Organisation Administrators (departments), and Developers (technical management).

Profile information (name, designation, education), learning activity (course enrolments, assessment scores), competency assessments, and AI chat sessions.
