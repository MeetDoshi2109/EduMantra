❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is EduMantra? ▾
EduMantra is an AI-enabled Skill Intelligence and Learning Platform designed for officials engaged in India's Official Statistical System. It assesses competencies, identifies skill gaps, and recommends personalized learning pathways from iGOT Karmayogi and NSSTA TPAC programmes.
How does the skill gap analysis work? ▾
When you create your profile (designation, education, experience, job role), our AI engine maps your competencies against predefined frameworks for Official Statistics. It compares your current level to requirements and generates a detailed gap report with severity ratings and LLM-powered recommendations.
Is EduMantra integrated with iGOT Karmayogi? ▾
Yes. EduMantra directly integrates with the iGOT Karmayogi platform. You can browse the course catalogue, enrol directly, track your progress and have completions reflected in your competency profile — all from within EduMantra.
What languages does the platform support? ▾
EduMantra supports English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali and Marathi. The AI assistant can respond in your preferred language, making the platform accessible to officials across all states.
How does the AI MCQ generator work? ▾
Educators can upload documents, presentations, or paste text content. Our AI (powered by GPT-4o) analyses the content and generates multiple-choice questions with options, correct answers, difficulty levels and detailed explanations — ready to publish as an assessment in minutes.
Is my data secure? ▾
Yes. EduMantra uses Supabase with Row Level Security (RLS) so each user can only access their own data. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. The platform supports role-based access control, SSO, and maintains full audit logs of all actions.
Who can use EduMantra? ▾
EduMantra is designed for five roles: Students/Officials (learners), Educators/Instructors (trainers), Supervisors (monitoring staff performance), Organisation Administrators (managing departments), and Developers (technical platform management).

Privacy Policy

Data We Collect
Profile information (name, designation, education), learning activity (course enrolments, assessment scores), competency assessments, and AI chat sessions.

How We Use It
To generate personalised competency profiles, identify skill gaps, recommend learning pathways, track progress and improve platform features.

All data is stored on Supabase-hosted PostgreSQL databases with row-level security encryption. Servers are hosted in compliance with Government of India data localisation requirements.
